## 📌 Notes & rationale

- **Why no `pokemon` in Apple keywords?** "Pokémon" is a trademark of The Pokémon Company. Apple has rejected listings that use it in keywords. We use it in the description (descriptive use is allowed) and rely on `pokedex`, `tcg`, `binder`, `tracker` for discovery.
- **Why "upcoming" / "Made for the Bindex binder" in promo text?** The physical Bindex binder is not yet on sale. Apple/Google can reject listings that mention products that don't exist. The promo text field is editable without re-review — swap to a launch announcement when the binder ships.
- **Why one-time IAP, not subscription?** Beginner-collector audience prefers pay-once. Subscriptions hurt conversion for utility apps and create monthly support overhead.
