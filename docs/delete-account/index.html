Delete Your Bindex Account

Permanently remove your account and all associated data.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Bindex app and log in.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the action. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your Bindex account and we'll delete it for you within 7 days.

Please include in your email:

  • Your account email address
  • A short note confirming you want the account deleted
Email admin@bindexofficial.com

What will be deleted

  • Your user profile (email, display name, preferences)
  • All of your binders
  • All tracked cards, progress, and custom cards
  • Your authentication credentials

What is kept

  • Anonymized or aggregated usage data that is no longer linked to you (e.g. crash reports), which we retain to keep the app working.
  • Records we are legally required to retain (e.g. billing records for purchases), retained only as long as required by law.

Important: Pro subscriptions

Deleting your Bindex account does not cancel an active Bindex Pro subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not Bindex.

To cancel your subscription:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Bindex → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bindex → Cancel.
